How billing works

🤖 AI Token Usage

Every AI interaction uses tokens. We use weighted tokens — output costs 4x more than input, so we count it as: input + (output × 4). One number to track, one overage rate. Simple.

Plan Included (weighted) Overage
Free (opt-in) 0 $1.20/M
Basic 5,000,000 $1.05/M
Pro 20,000,000 $0.75/M
Enterprise 50,000,000 $0.50/M

A typical shop using the AI daily burns about 2-3M weighted tokens per month. The included allowances cover normal use — overage only kicks in for heavy AI shops. Example: 3M input + 100K output = 3.4M weighted tokens. On Basic, that's under the 5M limit.

📱 SMS Messages

SMS covers appointment reminders, follow-up notifications, and service reminders. Each plan includes a monthly quota. Additional messages are $0.05 each.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your billing settings. When you upgrade, you get immediate access to the new features and we prorate the difference. When you downgrade, the change takes effect at the end of your billing period.

What's the early dev sale?

While automotAI is in early development, all paid plans are $10 off. It's our way of thanking early adopters. We'll give plenty of notice before the sale ends.

How does the free trial work?

14 days of full Pro access. No credit card required. At the end of the trial, choose a plan or continue on Free. Your data stays with you either way.

What happens if I hit my token limit?

On paid plans, the AI keeps working and overage is billed to your payment method on file. You can set a monthly spending cap in your billing settings. On Free, the AI stops until the next billing period — you'll need to upgrade or wait.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from your billing settings. Your subscription remains active until the end of the billing period. Annual plans get a prorated refund for unused months.

Do you charge platform fees on payments?

Yes. Free tier pays 3% per transaction. Basic and Pro pay 1.5%. Enterprise pays 1.0%. This covers Stripe processing and platform costs. The fee is shown on every invoice and payment confirmation.
